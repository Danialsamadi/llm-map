    How to use this map

    • Each dot is one AI model. Each line joins one company's models, oldest to newest.
    • Drag to turn the chart. Scroll, or pinch on a phone, to zoom.
    • Click a dot to see all of that model's numbers.
    • Drag the yellow figure into the chart to step inside it.
    • The link in your address bar keeps your question and companies, so you can share exactly what you see.

    What the measures mean

      Where the numbers come from

      Smartness, cost, price, speed and release dates come from Artificial Analysis. ARC-AGI-2 scores come from the ARC Prize leaderboard and are matched to the same model and effort setting; models ARC hasn't tested have no score.
